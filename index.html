About

I study how LLM-based agents reason, learn from feedback, and support healthcare decisions while remaining useful, interpretable, and trustworthy.

My work spans clinical-agent learning, affective computing, evidence appraisal, patient simulation, and evidence-grounded clinical decision support. I pair model development with careful ablation, error analysis, and human-in-the-loop evaluation.

Before joining UT Austin, I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Vehicle Engineering from Tongji University. That training continues to shape the systems-oriented way I approach clinical informatics and AI for healthcare research.

Research interests

  • LLMs & AI Agents Feedback-driven learning, post-training, skill revision, and multi-turn reasoning.
  • Affective computing Modeling and controlling emotional behavior in realistic virtual patients.
  • AI for healthcare Clinical evidence appraisal, decision support, simulation, and rigorous evaluation.

DxSkill: How Feedback Shapes Clinical Agents' Learning from Experience

A framework for studying how clinical agents revise diagnostic skills after an encounter using source-traceable guideline feedback and stage-wise skill fusion.

AI Health Lab · 2026

EmoPatient: Emotion-Adaptive Patient Simulation

An emotion-directed patient simulator with a closed-loop multi-agent architecture for generating dynamic, interpretable responses in palliative care communication.

Patient simulation · Multi-agent systems · Emotional modeling

AI Health Lab · 2026

Steerable Emotional Behavior in LLM Virtual Patients

An emotion-steering framework that uses prompting and contrastive activation-vector injection to control fear, sadness, and anger in multi-turn clinical dialogues.

Affective computing · Activation steering · Clinical fidelity

Submitted to IEEE ICHI 2027

UT Austin × Mayo Clinic · 2026

Same Diagnosis, Different Evidence Maps

A differential-evidence mapping framework that reveals substantial disagreement in clinical evidence appraisal across eight LLMs, even when they reach the same diagnosis.

Clinical reasoning · Evidence appraisal · Robust evaluation

Submitted to IEEE ICHI 2027

Dell Medical School · 2025-2026

Consensus-Anchored Clinical Decision Support for Long COVID

A multi-corpus clinical support system integrating expert guidance, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, and systematic reviews with traceable evidence synthesis.
